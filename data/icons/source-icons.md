# Bundled Icons

Service logos identify [Jellyfin (CC-BY-SA-4.0)](https://github.com/jellyfin/jellyfin), [Navidrome (GPL-3.0)](https://github.com/navidrome/navidrome), [OpenSubsonic (Apache-2.0)](https://github.com/opensubsonic/open-subsonic-api), [Emby](https://emby.media/), and [Plex](https://www.plex.tv/). WebDAV artwork comes from [Dashboard Icons](https://dashboardicons.com/icons/webdav), and Samba artwork from [samba.org](https://www.samba.org/samba/style/2010/samba_2010_logo_transparent_s_only_64x52.png).

External service logos come from [Last.fm](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Last.fm_favicon.png) and [MusicBrainz](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:MusicBrainz_Logo_Icon_(2016).svg).

Logos may be resized or adapted for display; their inclusion does not imply affiliation.

Interface icons include adaptations from [FolderPlay (GPL-3.0)](https://github.com/juancarlosbernal/FolderPlay), [Breeze Icons (LGPL-3.0-or-later)](https://invent.kde.org/frameworks/breeze-icons), and the [GNOME Icon Development Kit (CC0-1.0)](https://gitlab.gnome.org/Teams/Design/icon-development-kit), including its [archived catalog](https://gitlab.gnome.org/Teams/Design/icon-development-kit-www).
